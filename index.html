HTML: The Skeleton

HyperText Markup Language gives a webpage its structure.

  • Tags vs Elements: A tag <p> plus content is an element.
  • Meaning: Semantic tags tell the browser what the content *is*.
  • No Closing Tags: Known as "Void Elements" (e.g., <img>, <br>, <hr>).

CSS: The Beauty

  • Selectors: Targeting HTML by tag, .class, or #id.
  • Linking: Connecting via the <link rel="stylesheet"> tag in HTML.
  • Box Model: Mastering Margin (outside), Padding (inside), Borders, and Colors.

JS: The Brain

Making the webpage think and react.

  • Variables: Use let for values that change.
  • Constants: Use const for values that stay the same.
  • Data Types: Strings "Text", Numbers 123, and Booleans (true/false).

Domain Names

The "Address" of your digital home.

  • Definition: A human-readable string (like google.com) that points to an IP address.
  • Meaning: It is your identity on the web.
  • TLDs: Common endings include .com, .org, or .ng.

Web Hosting

The "Land" where your website lives.

  • Definition: Renting space on a powerful computer (server) that stays on 24/7.
  • Meaning: Without hosting, your website files stay on your laptop and can't be seen by the world.
